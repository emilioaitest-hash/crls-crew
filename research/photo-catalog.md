# CRLS Crew — Instagram Photo Catalog

Visual audit of all **41** JPGs in `assets/instagram/full/`. Every file was opened and looked at
individually; nothing here is inferred from filenames or captions.

## Headline finding

| | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Genuine photographs** | **8** | 20% |
| **Marketing graphics / posters / screenshots** | **33** | 80% |

Four fifths of the archive is designed collateral — race-announcement posters, registration
countdowns and results screenshots. **This archive cannot carry a high-end site on its own.**

The critical nuance: **~20 of those 33 graphics are text lockups sitting on top of genuinely
excellent photography.** The team clearly owns beautiful source frames — golden-hour bow-on
eights, the Boston skyline, drone aerials — but only ever published them with type burned in.
The single highest-leverage action for this project is to **ask the team for the clean originals**
(see the ask list at the end). That converts a thin archive into a strong one.

Only **three** genuine photographs are strong enough for prominent placement today:
`DY-7vl_HNW6`, `DOXG__QDz1p`, `DWt1oCdjjKJ` (the last with a caveat — it is MIT kit).

## Best full-bleed hero candidate

**`DOXG__QDz1p.jpg`** — of images usable *as they are*, this is the only real hero.

It is the sole frame combining **place, people and sport**: the white clapboard Cambridge Boat
Club fills the upper half with a warmly lit open bay and racked shells, while fourteen novice
girls and their coach hold a single oar straight across the composition. Dusk light gives it a
warm-interior-against-cool-sky palette (`#E8E6E1` / `#C89A3C` / `#7E8A96`) that will drive a
site's whole colour system. It has no burned-in text, it is square at 1440px so it crops to a
wide banner cleanly, and the horizontal oar is a natural line to hang headline type on. Caveats:
mild low-light softness, and it is a girls' novice group — pair it with a boys' frame elsewhere.

**If you can obtain one clean original, use `DIewzEcvQUv` or `DJFTbZhx_tU` instead.** Both are
the same spectacular golden-hour setup — a four coming bow-on, oars fanned and squared over
glassy copper water, sunset behind the tree line, Victorian boathouse at the right edge. Either
would outclass anything currently shippable. They are a 9.5 clean versus this 8.5.
